Track visitor engagement, monitor page views and link click rates, and generate branded QR codes for offline customer acquisition.

## Overview

Bio Analytics gives you clear insights into how effectively your bio profile converts Instagram followers into active link clicks and customers. The built-in QR Code Generator creates high-resolution scan codes suitable for packaging, flyers, pop-up stores, and business cards.

## What You Can Do

- View aggregate page visits and unique visitor counts.
- Track total link clicks across all active blocks.
- Inspect total successful Smart Redirect rule executions.
- Monitor active vs. total block capacity.
- Generate and download dynamic QR codes directly linked to your public bio URL.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Review Analytics
1. Navigate to the **Analytics** tab in the Link-in-Bio dashboard.
2. Review the top KPI cards:
   - **Total Views**: Visitor page loads on your public profile.
   - **Total Clicks**: Outbound interactions on your links and product cards.
   - **Redirect Hits**: Successful promo link searches.
   - **Active Blocks**: Count of currently published blocks.

### Step 2: Generate and Download QR Code
1. Click the **QR Code** button in the top action bar.
2. A high-resolution QR code is rendered for your profile URL.
3. Click **Download PNG** to save the image to your computer for marketing collateral.

## Metrics Breakdown

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Views Count** | Total times your public bio page was loaded by visitors. |
| **Clicks Count** | Total clicks across all interactive link blocks and product cards. |
| **Redirect Hits** | Total instances where visitors resolved a Reel or promo link via the Smart Input. |
| **Click-Through Rate (CTR)** | Ratio of total clicks to total page views. |

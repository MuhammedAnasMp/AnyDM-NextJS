# Link-in-Bio Overview

The Link-in-Bio feature allows you to build and host a high-converting, mobile-optimized creator profile page with custom links, product showcase cards, social channels, and intelligent URL redirection.

## Overview

Your AnyDM Link-in-Bio serves as the single public link for your Instagram bio (e.g., `yourdomain.com/@username`). It converts social traffic into customers by displaying active product catalogs, lead capture forms, external links, social handles, and an interactive Reel promo resolver.

## What You Can Do

- Customize your public username slug and verify real-time handle availability.
- Edit your public display title, biographical description, and avatar profile photo.
- Preview your bio page live across Mobile, Tablet, and Desktop device frames.
- Toggle page publishing status (Online / Draft).
- Share or copy your public profile URL with a single click.
- Download customized QR codes for offline marketing and print materials.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Open Link-in-Bio Editor
Navigate to **Link-in-Bio** from the main sidebar.

### Step 2: Configure Your Public Identity
1. In the top profile bar or **Settings** tab, enter your desired public **Username**.
2. Wait for the real-time availability check to show `Available`.
3. Set your **Display Title** and enter a compelling **Bio description**.
4. Upload your avatar photo and header cover banner.

### Step 3: Preview and Publish
1. Use the device toggle buttons (`Mobile`, `Tablet`, `Desktop`) above the phone simulator to preview how your profile renders.
2. Click **Save Changes** in the bottom bar.
3. Click **Copy Link** to paste into your Instagram bio.

## Available Options

| Option / Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| **Username Handle** | Your unique public URL slug (`/@username`). |
| **Profile Title** | Main display name shown at the top of your bio page. |
| **Bio Description** | Short summary or tagline introducing your brand. |
| **Profile Photo (Avatar)** | High-resolution circular profile image. |
| **Banner Image** | Top header cover image for your bio profile. |
| **Publish Status** | Toggle your bio page live or hidden from public access. |
| **Device Simulator** | Switch between Mobile (375px), Tablet (640px), and Desktop (100%) views. |
| **Copy Bio Link** | Copies your public link directly to the clipboard. |
| **QR Code Generator** | Generates high-resolution QR codes pointing to your bio page. |

# Bio Social Accounts

Connect and showcase your social channels, community groups, and streaming profiles directly on your Link-in-Bio page.

> **View in Application →** [Open Social Settings](/dashboard/bio)

![Screenshot placeholder: Bio Social Accounts](./images/bio-social.png)
> **Screenshot:** Link-in-Bio Social tab showing supported platform network icons, custom social channel adder, display mode options, and handle visibility switches.

## Overview

Social Accounts let your visitors connect with you across all platforms from one central hub. You can display official brand icons for major social networks, add custom links with dedicated icons, and choose where and how icons appear on your bio page.

## What You Can Do

- Add official profile links for top networks: Instagram, YouTube, X (Twitter), TikTok, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, GitHub, Spotify, Facebook, Twitch, Discord, Telegram, and Pinterest.
- Create custom channels with custom labels and icons.
- Toggle between display layouts (e.g., top header icon bar or dedicated link cards).
- Show or hide platform usernames/handles next to icons.
- Reorder your social channels.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Add Social Platforms
1. Open the **Social** tab in the Link-in-Bio dashboard.
2. Select a platform from the popular network list or click **+ Add Custom Social**.
3. Enter your handle or direct profile URL (e.g., `https://instagram.com/yourhandle`).
4. Click **Save**.

### Step 2: Configure Display Settings
1. Set the **Display Mode** to position icons at the top of your profile or embedded among your blocks.
2. Toggle **Show Social Usernames** if you want your handles visible beside each network icon.

### Step 3: Save and Verify
Click **Save Settings** in the bottom bar and verify the appearance on the device simulator.

## Supported Social Platforms

| Platform | Recommended Link Format |
|---|---|
| **Instagram** | `https://instagram.com/username` |
| **YouTube** | `https://youtube.com/@channel` |
| **X (Twitter)** | `https://x.com/username` |
| **WhatsApp** | `https://wa.me/phonenumber` |
| **TikTok** | `https://tiktok.com/@username` |
| **LinkedIn** | `https://linkedin.com/in/profile` |
| **Spotify** | `https://open.spotify.com/artist/id` |
| **Discord** | `https://discord.gg/invitecode` |
| **Telegram** | `https://t.me/channelname` |
| **GitHub** | `https://github.com/username` |
| **Custom Link** | Any valid web URL with a custom icon and label |

# Bio Themes & Styling

Customize the visual aesthetics, background palettes, glassmorphic themes, and typography of your Link-in-Bio profile.

> **View in Application →** [Open Link-in-Bio Styling](/dashboard/bio)

## Overview

The Styling engine lets creators match their bio page to their unique branding. Choose from pre-designed aesthetic themes or build a custom style using background gradients, custom wallpaper photos, dark/light overlays, and contrasting font colors.

## What You Can Do

- Select from built-in theme presets including *Glass Monochrome*, *Minimal*, *Creator*, *Dark Glass*, *Gradient Glow*, *Cyber Neon*, *Sunset Warmth*, *Clean White*, and *Luxury Gold*.
- Choose custom background modes: Solid Colors, Preset Gradients, or Custom Image Wallpapers.
- Adjust background overlay shading (*Dark*, *Heavy*, *Light*) for optimal legibility.
- Customize primary and secondary text colors.
- Upload high-resolution cover banners and avatar images.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Select a Theme Preset
1. In the Link-in-Bio editor, click on the **Styling** tab.
2. Browse through the available theme cards and click any preset to apply it immediately to the preview simulator.

### Step 2: Customize Background
1. Under **Background Type**, choose **Preset**, **Custom Color**, or **Image**.
2. If selecting **Image**, click **Upload Background Image** and choose an image from your device.
3. Select an overlay intensity (*Dark*, *Heavy*, or *Light*) to ensure text remains crisp and readable over background imagery.

### Step 3: Save Your Style
1. Review the live simulated render in the right-hand preview panel.
2. Click **Save Settings** to push changes to your public page.

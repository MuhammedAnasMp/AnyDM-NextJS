# Creator Hub

The Creator Hub is an exclusive portal for approved VIP Creators to track revenue sharing, manage affiliate cash commissions, and submit payout withdrawal requests.

> **View in Application →** [Open Creator Hub](/dashboard/creator)

![Screenshot placeholder: Creator Hub Dashboard](./images/creator-overview.png)
> **Screenshot:** Creator Hub dashboard displaying Total Earnings, Available Payout Balance, Pending Commission, 10% Revenue Share metric, and Bank Settlement withdrawal action.

## Overview

AnyDM Creator VIPs earn recurring cash commissions by referring paying subscribers and generating storefront sales. The Creator Hub tracks real-time affiliate earnings, shows payout settlement eligibility, and processes transfers directly to your verified bank account.

> [!NOTE]
> The Creator Hub is visible to users with an active Creator VIP or Affiliate partner status.

## What You Can Do

- Track financial balances: **Total Lifetime Earnings**, **Available for Payout**, and **Pending Balance**.
- Monitor affiliate commission conversion rates (10% standard revenue-share on referred subscription payments).
- Request direct bank payout settlements once the minimum payout threshold (₹500) is reached.
- Review historical transaction and payout records.
- Verify your Seller KYC status to ensure uninterrupted bank settlements.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Access the Creator Hub
Navigate to **Creator Hub** from the main sidebar.

### Step 2: Track Commission Earnings
Review the top metric cards:
- **Available Balance**: Cash ready to be withdrawn to your bank.
- **Pending Balance**: Commission currently in the clearing period.
- **Referred Pro Conversions**: Total paid subscribers attributed to your affiliate code.

### Step 3: Request a Payout Withdrawal
1. Once your available balance exceeds **₹500**, click the **Request Payout** button.
2. Confirm your verified bank account details (managed in Seller KYC).
3. Click **Submit Withdrawal Request**. Payouts are reviewed and settled by the finance team.

## Creator Program Parameters & Payout Rules

| Parameter | Value / Policy |
|---|---|
| **Commission Rate** | 10.0% recurring revenue share on referred paid subscriptions. |
| **Minimum Payout** | ₹500 minimum balance required to submit a withdrawal request. |
| **Settlement Method** | Direct Bank Transfer (NEFT / IMPS) or verified UPI ID. |
| **KYC Requirement** | Approved Seller KYC (PAN + Bank Account) is mandatory before funds are disbursed. |
| **Clearing Period** | Standard 7-day dispute window before pending funds become available for withdrawal. |

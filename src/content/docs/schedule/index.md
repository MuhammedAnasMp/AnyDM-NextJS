# Schedule Posts & Publisher

The Instagram Post Scheduler allows you to plan, create, preview, schedule, and automatically publish Instagram Reels, Images, Videos, Stories, and Carousels.

## Overview

AnyDM Scheduler connects directly to the official Meta Graph API to publish your content automatically without requiring push notifications or manual approvals. You can prepare content in advance, write engaging captions with AI assistance, configure custom reel cover thumbnails, and monitor publishing status in real time.

## What You Can Do

- Schedule multiple post formats: **Reels**, **Single Images**, **Videos**, **Stories**, and **Multi-Image Carousels**.
- Upload media directly with Cloudinary-backed high-speed cloud processing.
- Select or upload custom video cover thumbnails for Instagram Reels.
- Write captions with built-in emoji picker, hashtag tools, and AI caption generation.
- Choose **Publish Now** for immediate delivery or set a future publication date and time.
- Filter scheduled posts by status (`ALL`, `SCHEDULED`, `PUBLISHED`, `FAILED`, `DRAFT`) and media type.
- Preview posts in realistic Instagram Mobile and Desktop feed simulators before publishing.
- Edit, cancel, or reschedule pending posts.
- Inspect direct Instagram permalinks for published posts or view detailed error logs for failed attempts.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Create a New Post
1. Navigate to **Schedule Posts** from the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Schedule New Post**.
3. Select the content format: `REELS`, `IMAGE`, `VIDEO`, `STORIES`, or `CAROUSEL`.

### Step 2: Upload Media & Configure Thumbnail
1. Drag and drop your image or video files, or click to upload.
2. For Reels and Videos, select a specific video frame or upload a custom cover thumbnail image.
3. Toggle **Share to Feed** if you want your Reel visible on both your profile grid and the Reels tab.

### Step 3: Write Caption & Hashtags
1. Enter your post caption in the editor.
2. Use the **Emoji Picker** or click **AI Caption Assistant** to generate engaging, viral captions.
3. Add relevant hashtags to maximize reach.

### Step 4: Schedule or Publish
1. To post immediately, click **Publish Now**.
2. To schedule for later, choose your target **Date and Time**, then click **Schedule Post**.
3. Monitor your post status on the calendar and feed list.

## Available Options & Post Types

| Option / Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Reels (`REELS`)** | Full-screen vertical video posts published to your Instagram Reels feed and profile. |
| **Carousel (`CAROUSEL`)** | Multi-slide posts supporting multiple uploaded images in a swipeable sequence. |
| **Images & Video (`IMAGE` / `VIDEO`)** | Standard feed photo and video posts. |
| **Stories (`STORIES`)** | 24-hour ephemeral story posts. |
| **Custom Cover Thumbnail** | Select an exact video timestamp or upload an image as your reel cover. |
| **Share to Feed Toggle** | Controls whether a published Reel is also placed on your main profile feed grid. |
| **Publishing Status** | Live status indicators: `DRAFT`, `SCHEDULED`, `PROCESSING`, `PUBLISHED`, `FAILED`, `CANCELLED`. |
| **Instagram Permalink** | Direct link to open and view the live published post on Instagram. |

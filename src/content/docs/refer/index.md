# Refer & Earn

Earn reward points by inviting creators and friends to AnyDM, unlock free monthly extensions of Creator Pro, and track your referral network.

## Overview

The AnyDM Referral Program rewards you for sharing the platform with other creators. Every successful referral earns reward points that can be redeemed for free months of the Creator Pro plan or converted into milestone badges.

## What You Can Do

- View and customize your personal unique **Referral Code** and invite link (`/signup?ref=CODE`).
- Copy and share your referral link across social media, groups, and direct messages.
- Track real-time referral metrics: Total Points Earned, Successful Signups, and Pro Days Claimed.
- Redeem 100 Reward Points for 1 Month of Free Creator Pro access.
- Complete bonus social engagement tasks (e.g., Follow AnyDM on Instagram) to earn instant bonus points.
- Inspect the referral history log showing referred users, signup dates, and point allocation status.
- Enter a friend's referral code if you signed up without one.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Share Your Referral Link
1. Navigate to **Refer & Earn** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Copy Link** to copy your personalized invitation URL.
3. Share your link with creators, influencers, and digital businesses.

### Step 2: Customize Your Referral Code
1. Click the **Pencil / Edit** icon next to your referral code.
2. Enter your custom vanity code (e.g., `ALEX2026`).
3. Click **Save** to update your code and link.

### Step 3: Redeem Points for Free Pro
1. When your balance reaches **100 points**, the **Redeem 1 Month Pro** button becomes active.
2. Click **Redeem Now** to instantly extend your Pro membership subscription by 30 days without charging your card.

### Step 4: Claim Social Bonus Points
- Click the **Follow on Instagram (+10 pts)** task card.
- Follow `@anydm.in` and click **Verify & Claim** to receive your bonus points.

## Reward Points Economy & Milestones

| Action / Tier | Reward Value |
|---|---|

| **Instagram Social Task** | +10 Reward Points (one-time bonus upon verification). |
| **1 Month Pro Extension** | Costs 100 Reward Points (Extends subscription by 30 days). |
| **Bronze Tier** | 1–3 Successful Referrals. |
| **Silver Tier** | 4–9 Successful Referrals. |
| **Gold Ambassador Tier** | 10+ Successful Referrals with priority support perks. |

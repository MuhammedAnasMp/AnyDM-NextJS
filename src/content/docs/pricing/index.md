Explore AnyDM membership tiers, upgrade to Creator Pro for unlimited DM automations and AI tokens, and manage your billing cycle.

## Overview

AnyDM offers a transparent freemium model. New creators begin with a full-featured Free Trial to experience automated direct messages and storefront capabilities. Upgrading to **Creator Pro** unlocks unlimited high-speed DM triggers, master AI model access, unlimited product listings, and priority webhook processing.

## What You Can Do

- Compare feature availability between the **Free Trial** and **Creator Pro** plans.
- Check current subscription status, active plan tier, and trial days remaining.
- Upgrade or renew your Creator Pro subscription seamlessly via Razorpay (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking).
- Redeem earned reward points for free 30-day Pro plan extensions.
- View upcoming billing dates and payment receipts.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Review Plan Comparison
1. Open **Pricing** from the main sidebar.
2. Review the plan comparison matrix to select the tier that matches your business needs.

### Step 2: Upgrade to Creator Pro
1. Click the **Upgrade to Pro (₹499/mo)** button on the Pro plan card.
2. The secure **Razorpay Checkout** popup will appear.
3. Select your preferred payment method (UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Cards, or Netbanking).
4. Complete payment. Your account is upgraded instantly, and all Pro features activate immediately.

### Step 3: Extend Subscription Using Points
- If you have accumulated **100+ Reward Points** through referrals, click **Extend with 100 Points** to add 30 free days of Pro access to your account.

# Seller KYC & Bank Verification

Complete mandatory identity verification, submit your PAN and Aadhaar details, and connect your bank account to receive customer order payouts.

> **View in Application →** [Open Seller KYC](/dashboard/settings/kyc)

## Overview

To comply with Indian financial regulations and enable direct bank settlement payouts for products sold via AnyDM, creators must submit their Seller KYC (Know Your Customer) details. Once verified by our compliance team, sales revenue and affiliate earnings are transferred directly to your bank account.

## What You Can Do

- Submit your legal Full Name matching your bank and tax documents.
- Enter your Permanent Account Number (**PAN**).
- Enter your **Aadhaar Number** for identity confirmation.
- Enter your receiving **Bank Account Number**, Confirm Account Number, and **Bank IFSC Code**.
- Track real-time verification status: `PENDING`, `SUBMITTED`, `APPROVED`, or `REJECTED`.
- Receive bank settlement payouts for customer store purchases and creator commissions.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Open KYC Form
Navigate to **Settings > Seller KYC** from the sidebar.

### Step 2: Fill Identity Information
1. Enter your **Legal Full Name** exactly as it appears on your bank account and PAN card.
2. Enter your 10-character **PAN Number** (e.g., `ABCDE1234F`).
3. Enter your 12-digit **Aadhaar Number**.

### Step 3: Enter Bank Account Details
1. Enter your **Bank Name** (e.g., *HDFC Bank, State Bank of India, ICICI*).
2. Enter your **Bank Account Number**.
3. Re-enter your account number in **Confirm Account Number**.
4. Enter the 11-character **Bank IFSC Code** (e.g., `HDFC0001234`).

### Step 4: Submit for Verification
1. Review all entered details for precision.
2. Click **Submit KYC for Verification**.
3. Your application is submitted to the compliance queue. Approvals are typically completed within 24–48 business hours.

## Available KYC Fields & Verification Statuses

| Field / Status | Description |
|---|---|
| **Legal Full Name** | Full name registered with tax authorities and bank records. |
| **PAN Number** | 10-digit Permanent Account Number required for tax reporting. |
| **Aadhaar Number** | 12-digit national identity number. |
| **Bank Account Number** | Bank destination account for electronic fund transfers (NEFT/IMPS). |
| **Bank IFSC** | Indian Financial System Code identifying your specific bank branch. |
| **Status: PENDING** | KYC form has not yet been submitted. |
| **Status: SUBMITTED** | Under active review by compliance administrators. |
| **Status: APPROVED** | KYC approved. Automatic payout settlements are active. |
| **Status: REJECTED** | Discrepancy detected. Review feedback notes and resubmit corrected info. |

# Workspace & Team Settings

Configure organization identity, workspace display name, default timezones, and team member permission tiers.

> **View in Application →** [Open Workspace Settings](/dashboard/settings/workspace)

## Overview

The Workspace Settings tab allows you to organize your business workspace. Whether you are an individual creator or an agency managing social commerce for multiple brands, your workspace holds your team credentials, settings, and business profile.

## What You Can Do

- Update your Workspace Display Name.
- Review your current plan tier's Team Size Limit (e.g., 5 member seats on Creator Pro).
- Configure default workspace timezones for accurate post scheduling.
- Save organization branding preferences.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Open Workspace Settings
Navigate to **Settings > Workspace** from the main sidebar.

### Step 2: Edit Workspace Details
1. Update your **Workspace Name** (e.g., *"Alex Rivera Media Studio"*).
2. Review your team seat allocation.
3. Click **Save Settings** to confirm.

## Available Workspace Options

| Option / Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| **Workspace Name** | Organization or business name displayed in navigation headers and invoices. |
| **Team Size Limit** | Maximum team member seats supported under your active subscription plan. |
| **Plan Status** | Shows active plan tier (Free Trial or Creator Pro). |

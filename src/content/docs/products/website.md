# Creator Storefront Website

Configure and personalize your standalone e-commerce storefront website powered by AnyDM.

> **View in Application →** [Open Creator Website](/dashboard/products/website)

![Screenshot placeholder: Creator Storefront Settings](./images/products-website.png)
> **Screenshot:** Creator Storefront customization page showing domain options, brand logo uploader, hero banner customizer, featured products selector, and live store preview.

## Overview

AnyDM automatically generates a dedicated creator storefront website where your complete product catalog is hosted. Customers can browse all your digital goods, courses, physical products, and services in a responsive e-commerce environment.

## What You Can Do

- Customize your store's public brand name, logo, and announcement banner.
- Configure custom store domain or use your standard AnyDM creator URL (`username.anydm.in`).
- Feature specific hero products at the top of your shop.
- Organize products into browsable store collections and categories.
- Customize store policies: Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Refund Policy.
- Preview your live creator storefront on mobile and desktop views.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Open Storefront Settings
Navigate to **Products > Website** from the sidebar.

### Step 2: Configure Store Branding
1. Enter your **Store Name** and tagline.
2. Upload your high-resolution **Store Logo** and **Hero Banner**.
3. Set an **Announcement Banner** message (e.g., *"Free shipping on orders above ₹999!"* or *"Use code LAUNCH20 for 20% off!"*).

### Step 3: Curate Featured Products
1. Select which catalog items appear in the **Featured Collection** on your homepage.
2. Arrange product display order.

### Step 4: Review and Publish
1. Review the storefront preview on the right.
2. Click **Save & Update Store**.
3. Click **Visit Storefront** to open your live shop in a new tab.

## Available Storefront Settings

| Setting | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Store Name** | Main branding title shown in header, SEO titles, and receipts. |
| **Store Logo & Favicon** | Visual identity displayed across your storefront. |
| **Hero Banner** | Prominent graphic introducing your brand on the store homepage. |
| **Announcement Bar** | Top banner for sitewide alerts, discount codes, or news. |
| **Featured Products** | Hand-picked products prioritized on your storefront home page. |
| **Custom Domain** | Connect your own custom domain (e.g., `shop.yourbrand.com`). |
| **Legal Pages** | Editable Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Refund guidelines. |

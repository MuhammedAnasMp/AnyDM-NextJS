# Product Catalog

The Product Catalog is where you manage your digital products, e-books, courses, services, physical goods, and consultation offerings sold through Instagram DMs and your store.

> **View in Application →** [Open Product Catalog](/dashboard/products/catalog)

![Screenshot placeholder: Product Catalog](./images/products-catalog.png)
> **Screenshot:** Product Catalog dashboard showing product cards, pricing, stock levels, category filters, search bar, and Instagram import modal trigger.

## Overview

AnyDM turns your Instagram profile into a social commerce store. The Product Catalog houses all your sellable items, complete with checkout links, automated direct message triggers, pricing tiers, and delivery files.

## What You Can Do

- Browse, search, and filter all products in your inventory.
- Import products and media directly from your Instagram posts and reels.
- Add new digital downloads, services, courses, or physical items.
- Edit product details, pricing, discount rates, inventory, and delivery assets.
- Toggle product availability (Active / Inactive) or delete products.
- Quick-copy public product purchase links to share in chat or social bios.
- Track total orders and sales generated by each product.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Access the Catalog
Navigate to **Products > All Products** from the sidebar.

### Step 2: Search and Filter Items
- Use the **Search Bar** to find products by title or description.
- Use the **Filter Tabs** (`All`, `Digital`, `Physical`, `Course`, `Service`) to narrow down items.

### Step 3: Manage Products
- Click the **Pencil / Edit** icon on any product card to modify pricing, description, or files.
- Click the **Trash** icon to delete a product.
- Click **Copy Link** to paste the checkout URL into DMs or social posts.

### Step 4: Import from Instagram
Click **Import from Instagram** to pull media and captions from your published Instagram posts and convert them into store products automatically.

## Product Types & Catalog Attributes

| Attribute / Action | Description |
|---|---|
| **Product Type** | Digital Download (PDF/ZIP), Physical Good, Video Course, or 1-on-1 Consultation Service. |
| **Price & Currency** | Regular selling price and optional discounted comparison price. |
| **Stock / Inventory** | Inventory count tracking or unlimited digital delivery mode. |
| **Active Status** | Toggle whether the product is currently purchasable by customers. |
| **Direct Checkout Link** | Public link leading directly to the AnyDM seamless checkout screen. |
| **Orders Counter** | Total completed purchases recorded for this item. |

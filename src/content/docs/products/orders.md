# Orders & Fulfillment

Manage customer purchases, track payment statuses, monitor sales earnings, inspect order details, and oversee fulfillment.

> **View in Application →** [Open Orders](/dashboard/products/orders)

## Overview

The Orders dashboard provides comprehensive transactional management for your AnyDM digital store. Whenever a customer completes checkout via direct messages, bio links, or your creator website, the order is logged here with customer contact details, items purchased, transaction IDs, and settlement status.

## What You Can Do

- View financial summaries: Today's Sales, Total Earnings, Completed Orders, and Pending Payout Settlement.
- Search orders by Customer Name, Email, Order ID, or Product Title.
- Filter orders by status (`All`, `Pending`, `Completed`, `Cancelled`, `Refunded`).
- Inspect individual order details (buyer phone number, shipping address for physical goods, delivery status, and payment method).
- Update fulfillment status (Mark as Shipped / Completed).
- Copy tracking URLs and customer info for communication.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Review Sales Metrics
At the top of the Orders page, check your store performance metrics:
- **Today's Sales**: Gross revenue collected today.
- **Pending Orders**: Orders awaiting review or physical dispatch.
- **Completed Orders**: Successfully processed and delivered transactions.
- **Pending Settlement**: Earnings queued for transfer to your verified bank account.

### Step 2: Search and Inspect Orders
1. Enter customer name, email, or order number into the search bar.
2. Click on any order row in the table to open the **Order Details Modal**.
3. Review purchased items, unit price, quantity, buyer details, and payment confirmation.

### Step 3: Manage Fulfillment
- For digital goods: Delivery is instant and automated. The system records file download access.
- For physical goods: Review shipping address, pack items, and update status to `Shipped` or `Delivered`.

## Available Order Fields & Statuses

| Field / Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Order ID** | Unique transaction identifier (e.g., `#ORD-9281`). |
| **Customer Info** | Buyer name, email address, phone number, and Instagram handle if available. |
| **Product & Qty** | Purchased item title, variant, and quantity ordered. |
| **Total Amount** | Gross order payment amount including any applicable taxes and fees. |
| **Payment Status** | `PAID` (Payment captured), `PENDING` (Checkout initiated), `FAILED`, or `REFUNDED`. |
| **Fulfillment Status** | `COMPLETED` (Files delivered / fulfilled) or `PROCESSING`. |
| **Date & Time** | Timestamp of transaction creation. |

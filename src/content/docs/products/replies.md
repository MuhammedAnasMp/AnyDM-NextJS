# Product Auto-Replies

Configure automated Instagram Direct Message replies and public comment responses linked directly to your catalog products.

> **View in Application →** [Open Product Replies](/dashboard/products/replies)

![Screenshot placeholder: Product Auto-Replies](./images/products-replies.png)
> **Screenshot:** Product Auto-Replies management page showing product response cards, mapped keywords, custom DM text templates, and public comment reply variations.

## Overview

Product Auto-Replies connect your product catalog with AnyDM's Instagram automation engine. When users express interest in a specific product by commenting on your reels or messaging your inbox, these tailored templates automatically dispatch the product's image, description, and direct purchase link.

## What You Can Do

- Map specific products to automated comment trigger keywords (e.g., product name or code).
- Customize the direct message card format sent to interested buyers.
- Define multiple randomized public comment reply variations to maintain natural engagement.
- Include direct checkout buttons, discount codes, and product links.
- Toggle auto-replies on or off per product.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Select a Product
1. Navigate to **Products > Quick Replies**.
2. Select the catalog product you want to configure automation for.

### Step 2: Define Trigger Keywords
1. Enter the trigger keywords associated with this product (e.g., `DRESS, BUY, DETAILS, STORE`).
2. Set matching sensitivity to trigger whenever these words appear in comments on your posts.

### Step 3: Customize Message Templates
1. Edit the **DM Response Text**: Include greeting, product highlights, and a call-to-action.
2. Ensure the **Product Checkout Link** button is enabled.
3. Add 3–5 **Public Comment Reply Variations** (e.g., *"Just sent the details to your DMs! 👗"*, *"Check your inbox for the link! ✨"*).

### Step 4: Activate Auto-Replies
Toggle the rule status to **Active** and click **Save Changes**.

## Available Reply Settings

| Setting / Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Associated Product** | The specific catalog item linked to this auto-response flow. |
| **Trigger Keywords** | Keywords that activate this reply when detected in comments or DMs. |
| **DM Template** | Customizable direct message sent privately to the user. |
| **Checkout Button Text** | CTA button text (e.g., *"Buy Now - ₹499"* or *"View Product"*). |
| **Comment Variations** | List of public comment replies rotated randomly to avoid repetitive spam detection. |
| **Rule Status** | Master toggle to enable or pause automated replies for this product. |

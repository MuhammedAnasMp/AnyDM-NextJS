# Unified DM Live Chat

The Unified DM Live Chat is a fast, multi-threaded communication workspace for chatting with Instagram followers, managing customer support conversations, and taking over bot interactions.

## Overview

The AnyDM Inbox aggregates all your Instagram Direct Messages into a synchronized desktop workspace. You can seamlessly switch between automated AI responses and human agent interventions, send product cards and photos, view customer order histories, and resolve customer queries quickly.

## What You Can Do

- Manage multiple concurrent Instagram direct message conversations in real time.
- Search chat conversations by customer name, username handle, or message keywords.
- Send rich text, emojis, quick replies, product checkout cards, and media attachments (photos and playable videos).
- Toggle bot automation on or off for individual customer threads (*Human Takeover Mode*).
- Inspect customer profile details, follow status, total interactions, and lead score in the side panel.
- Filter conversations by tags, unread status, or lead stage.
- Delete or archive obsolete message threads.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Select a Conversation Thread
1. Navigate to **Inbox** from the main sidebar.
2. In the left-hand thread list, click on any customer conversation to load their chat history.
3. Unread messages and new inbound inquiries are highlighted at the top of the queue.

### Step 2: Send Messages & Product Cards
1. Type your response in the message input at the bottom of the chat window.
2. Click the **Product / Shopping Bag** icon to quickly attach a product checkout card.
3. Click the **Media** icon to upload and send images or video attachments.
4. Press Enter or click **Send** to dispatch the message directly into the customer's Instagram DM inbox.

### Step 3: Enable Human Takeover
- When a complex query requires manual support, click the **Pause Bot / Takeover** button at the top of the chat.
- While paused, automated bots will not trigger on this user's messages, allowing you to converse naturally.
- Click **Resume Bot** when finished to re-enable automated flows.

## Available Chat Controls & Indicators

| Control / Indicator | Function |
|---|---|
| **Thread List** | List of all Instagram direct message conversations with live snippet previews. |
| **Search Filter** | Find specific customer chats by name, handle, or recent message content. |
| **Bot Status Toggle** | Switch between automated bot handling and manual agent takeover. |
| **Product Card Inserter** | Instantly embed a catalog product card with a clickable checkout CTA into the chat. |
| **Media Player** | In-app player for previewing video and voice note attachments sent or received. |
| **Customer Info Sidebar** | Shows follower status, lead score, previous orders, and 24-hour window countdown. |
| **Refresh Stream** | Manually synchronizes conversation updates with Instagram servers. |
